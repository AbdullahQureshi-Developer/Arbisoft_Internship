== Company Research Brief ==

Company Name: Apple Inc.
Ticker Symbol: AAPL
Stock Exchange: NASDAQ
Industry: Consumer Electronics, Software, Online Services
Headquarters: One Apple Park Way, Cupertino, California, USA
Founded: April 1, 1976
Founders: Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, Ronald Wayne

== Leadership ==

Current CEO: Tim Cook (took over from Steve Jobs in 2011)
Current CFO: Luca Maestri
Current Chief Design Officer: Alan Dye
Board Chairman: Arthur Levinson

== Products and Services ==

Hardware:
- iPhone: Apple's flagship smartphone, first launched in 2007 by Steve Jobs.
- Mac: Personal computers running macOS, including MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, and Mac Pro.
- iPad: Tablet computers running iPadOS.
- Apple Watch: Smartwatch launched in 2015, now the world's best-selling watch.
- AirPods: Wireless earphones launched in 2016, now a category leader.
- Apple Vision Pro: Mixed reality headset launched in 2024 at $3,499.

Software and Services:
- iOS / macOS / watchOS / tvOS / visionOS: Apple's operating systems.
- App Store: Digital distribution platform for apps, launched in 2008.
- Apple Music: Streaming music service with over 100 million songs.
- Apple TV+: Video streaming service launched in 2019.
- iCloud: Cloud storage and sync service.
- Apple Pay: Mobile payment system launched in 2014.
- Siri: AI voice assistant, first introduced on iPhone 4S in 2011.
- Apple Intelligence: Apple's generative AI platform, announced in 2024.

== Financials ==

Revenue (FY 2023): $383 billion USD
Net Income (FY 2023): $97 billion USD
Market Capitalization: Approximately $3 trillion USD (as of 2024)
Number of Employees: Over 150,000 full-time employees worldwide
Apple became the first U.S. company to reach a $1 trillion market cap in 2018.
Apple became the first company globally to reach $3 trillion market cap in 2022.

== Key Historical Milestones ==

1976: Apple Computer Company founded in a garage in Los Altos, California.
1984: Apple Macintosh launched — the first mass-market personal computer with a GUI.
1997: Steve Jobs returned to Apple after being ousted; Apple nearly went bankrupt before his return.
1998: iMac launched, beginning Apple's design renaissance.
2001: iPod launched, transforming the music industry. iTunes Store launched in 2003.
2007: iPhone launched, revolutionizing the smartphone industry.
2010: iPad launched, creating the modern tablet market.
2011: Steve Jobs passed away on October 5, 2011. Tim Cook became CEO.
2020: Apple switched Mac computers from Intel processors to custom Apple Silicon (M1 chip).
2023: Apple Vision Pro announced, entering the spatial computing market.
2024: Apple Intelligence (on-device AI) announced at WWDC 2024.

== Competitors ==

Main competitors include:
- Samsung Electronics (smartphones, tablets, chips)
- Google / Alphabet (Android OS, Pixel phones, cloud services)
- Microsoft (software, cloud, Surface devices)
- Amazon (cloud services, Echo devices)
- Meta (social media, VR headsets like Quest)

== Supply Chain and Manufacturing ==

Apple designs all its products in California but outsources manufacturing primarily to:
- Foxconn (Hon Hai Precision): Main iPhone assembler based in Taiwan/China.
- TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company): Manufactures Apple's custom chips (A-series, M-series).
- Broadcom: Supplies Wi-Fi and Bluetooth chips; Apple signed a multi-year supply extension through 2031.
