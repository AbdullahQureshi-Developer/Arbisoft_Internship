
# Company Policy

## Working Hours

- Standard office hours are **9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**, Monday through Friday.
- Employees should be available during core working hours unless approved otherwise.

## Attendance

- Employees must inform their manager if they will be late or absent.
- Repeated unexcused absences may result in disciplinary action.

## Leave Policy

- Employees receive **20 paid annual leave days** per calendar year.
- Sick leave is available upon notifying the manager as soon as possible.
- Leave requests should be submitted at least **3 days in advance**, except for emergencies.

## Remote Work

- Employees may work remotely with prior approval from their manager.
- Remote employees must remain reachable during working hours.

## Code of Conduct

- Treat colleagues with professionalism and respect.
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior will not be tolerated.
- Company information must remain confidential.

## IT and Security

- Do not share passwords or API keys.
- Lock your computer when away from your desk.
- Only install approved software on company devices.

## Internet Usage

- Company internet should primarily be used for work-related activities.
- Excessive personal use during working hours should be avoided.
