
# Student Handbook

## Academic Integrity

Students are expected to complete their own work. Plagiarism, cheating, and unauthorized collaboration are strictly prohibited.

## Attendance

Students should attend all scheduled classes and labs. Excessive absences may affect grades.

## Assignment Submission

- Assignments must be submitted before the deadline.
- Late submissions may receive a grade penalty unless an extension has been approved.

## Examination Rules

- Students must present a valid student ID during examinations.
- Electronic devices are not allowed unless explicitly permitted.

## Grading Scale

| Percentage | Grade |
| ---------- | ----- |
| 90–100    | A     |
| 80–89     | B     |
| 70–79     | C     |
| 60–69     | D     |
| Below 60   | F     |

## Library

- Students may borrow up to **5 books** at a time.
- Loan period is **14 days**.
- Late returns may incur fines.

## Campus Facilities

Students have access to:

- Computer Labs
- Library
- Sports Complex
- Cafeteria
- Student Counseling Center

## Student Support

Support services include:

- Academic Advising
- Career Counseling
- Mental Health Counseling
- IT Help Desk

## Code of Conduct

Students are expected to:

- Respect faculty and fellow students.
- Maintain academic honesty.
- Follow university rules and regulations.
- Use campus facilities responsibly.
