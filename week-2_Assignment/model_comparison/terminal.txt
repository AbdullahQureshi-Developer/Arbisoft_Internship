│ Productivity is rarely about working *more* hours; it is about managing your **energy** and **attention**. To stay productive, you need a system that moves you from     │
│ reactive mode (responding to emails and pings) to proactive mode (doing deep, important work).                                                                           │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ Here is a framework for planning your day, broken down into stages:                                                                                                      │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ ---                                                                                                                                                                      │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ ### 1. The Night Before: The "Shutdown Ritual"                                                                                                                           │
│ The biggest mistake people make is starting their day by deciding what to do. This wastes your peak morning willpower on decision-making.                                │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ *   **The Brain Dump:** Write down everything swirling in your head. Once it’s on paper, your brain stops using energy to "loop" the thought.                            │
│     *   **The Rule of 3:** From your list, pick exactly **three** tasks that must get done tomorrow. If you only did these three, would you feel successful?             │
│     *   **Prep the Environment:** Lay out your clothes, pack your bag, or clear your desk. Reduce the number of decisions you have to make the next morning.             │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ ### 2. The Morning: High-Leverage Work                                                                                                                                   │
│ Most people spend their mornings "clearing the decks" (checking email, Slack, or news). This puts you in a reactive state.                                               │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ *   **Eat the Frog:** Do your hardest, most cognitively demanding task first. This is when your willpower and focus are at their peak.                                   │
│ *   **Deep Work Blocks:** Schedule 90-minute blocks of uninterrupted time. No phone, no notifications, no multitasking. Multitasking is a myth; it is actually "context  │
│ switching," which drains your brain power.                                                                                                                               │
│ *   **Avoid the "Inbox Trap":** Do not open your email until you have completed at least one significant task.                                                           │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ ### 3. The Afternoon: The "Slump" Management                                                                                                                             │
│ Most humans experience a circadian dip in energy between 2:00 PM and 4:00 PM.                                                                                            │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ *   **Low-Cognitive Tasks:** Save administrative tasks, filing, expense reports, or returning phone calls for this window.                                               │
│ *   **Movement:** Instead of reaching for more caffeine, take a 10-minute walk. It resets your focus better than a snack.                                                │
│ *   **Time Boxing:** If your afternoon feels chaotic, use "Time Boxing." Assign a specific time slot to every task (e.s., "1:00–1:30: Respond to emails"). This prevents │
│ tasks from bleeding into each other.                                                                                                                                     │
│                                                                                                                                                                          │

│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ *   **A Calendar:** For appointments and time-blocking (where you go).                                                                                                   │
│ *   **A To-Do List:** For tasks (what you do).                                                                                                                           │

│                                                                                                                                                                          │
│ #### A. The 2-Minute Rule                                                                                                                                                │
│ If a task comes up that takes less than two minutes (like replying to a quick "yes/no" email or putting a dish in the dishwasher), **do it immediately.** Managing it on │
